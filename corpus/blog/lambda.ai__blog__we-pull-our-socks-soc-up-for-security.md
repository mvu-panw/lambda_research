# We Pull Our Socks (SOC) Up For Security

 February 27, 2025

 • 1 min read

Lambda is excited to launch a new Security page, designed to be your go-to resource for understanding our commitment to safeguarding your data, as well as providing a highly secure cloud environment.

We’ll continue to add to it, and we'll have it linked under "Company" in the navigation menu at the top of the website, as well as in the footer.

Central to the page is the ability to request and receive by email Lambda’s SOC 2 Type II report: this report isn't just a checkbox; it's a testament to our rigorous auditing process, ensuring we meet the highest security standards. It's our commitment to you that your AI workloads are in good hands. 

Also available on the page are avenues to contact us for discovered vulnerabilities and reporting abuse, together with links to our Privacy Policy.

Security is everyone's responsibility, and we're here to lead the way. Explore our Security page, request to download the SOC2 Type II report, and let us know what you think!
