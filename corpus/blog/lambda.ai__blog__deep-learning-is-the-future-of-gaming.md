# Deep learning is the future of gaming.

 January 4, 2022

 (Updated on July 24, 2023)

 • 1 min read

Deep learning is the most important technology to impact gaming since the advent of 3D graphics. This short video presentation walks you through a few of the technologies that will deliver unbelievable gaming experiences in the near future.

### Research covered in this presentation:

- Photorealistic neural rendering

- Deepfakes for gaming

- Dreaming up game engines with neural networks

- Automating storytelling with large language models

- OpenAI Codex to automatically program games

- Large language models for building realistic NPCs
