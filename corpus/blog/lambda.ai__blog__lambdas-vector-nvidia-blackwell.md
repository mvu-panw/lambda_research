# Lambda’s Vector lineup gets a major GPU upgrade with NVIDIA Blackwell

 May 15, 2025

 (Updated on October 22, 2026)

 • 3 min read

The future of AI demands more power—and it’s here.

Lambda is proud to announce that our Vector One, Vector, and Vector Pro product lines have all been updated with NVIDIA Blackwell architecture. Whether you're training cutting-edge models, pushing the limits of inference, or experimenting with multimodal research, our systems are now equipped to take your work even further.

Here’s what’s new across the Lambda desktop and workstation lineups:

## Vector One: Now Shipping with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPU

Vector One, our flagship single-GPU desktop, now ships with the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPU—NVIDIA’s most powerful consumer GPU yet. Built for developers and researchers who need maximum performance in a mid-sized form factor, the GeForce RTX 5090 GPU delivers unmatched throughput for real-time inference, and generative AI workloads with an outstanding 32 GB of GDDR7 memory. Multiply performance with NVIDIA DLSS 4, generate images at unprecedented speed, and unleash creativity with NVIDIA Studio. Plus, access NVIDIA NIM microservices – state-of-the-art AI models that let enthusiasts and developers build AI assistants, agents, and workflows with peak performance on NIM-ready systems. Whether you’re iterating locally or running experiments before scaling to our cloud, Vector One with NVIDIA Blackwell keeps your workflow moving fast.

## Vector: Dual NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPUs, Liquid-Cooled

Orders for the new dual-GPU Vector desktop are now live and shipping. This workhouse is powered by two NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPUs, each cooled by a dedicated 360mm AIO liquid cooler, ensuring peak performance without the noise. Designed for engineers who need serious power without breaking the bank, the new Vector is perfect for large batch training, deep reinforcement learning, and multi-GPU workflows—right at your desk.

## Vector Pro: Now Featuring NVIDIA RTX PRO Blackwell GPUs

For teams running production workloads and enterprise-grade development environments, Vector Pro has been updated with the new RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Workstation Edition, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Max-Q Workstation Edition, RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell, RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell, and NVIDIA RTX PRO 4000 Blackwell GPUs. These updated professional-grade GPUs offer up to 96 GB of GDDR7 ECC memory, MIG support on the NVIDIA RTX PRO 5000 and 6000 Blackwell GPUs, and the long-lifecycle stability required in enterprise settings. Built for scalable deep learning, simulation, and high-end AI research, the new Vector Pro with NVIDIA RTX PRO Blackwell GPUs is now available for quoting.

At Lambda, we build systems engineered for AI. Our hardware is designed to meet the needs of researchers and ML engineers who don’t want to compromise on performance, thermals, noise, or reliability.

Whether you're just starting out or deploying at scale, NVIDIA Blackwell-powered systems from Lambda keep you at the edge of what’s possible.

Explore Vector, Vector Pro and Vector One, orcontact our team to learn more.
