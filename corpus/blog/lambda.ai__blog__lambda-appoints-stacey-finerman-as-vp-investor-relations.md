# Lambda Appoints Stacey Finerman as VP, Investor Relations

 October 21, 2025

 (Updated on October 22, 2026)

 • 2 min read

Seasoned IR Leader from Zayo Group, Marqeta, and Square Brings Deep Expertise 

SAN FRANCISCO, CA - OCTOBER 21, 2025 - Lambda, the Superintelligence Cloud, today announced the appointment of Stacey Finerman as VP, Investor Relations. Finerman brings over a decade of experience in financial communications and capital markets strategy to support Lambda's next stage of growth as a leader in AI infrastructure. 

"We’re delighted to have Stacey join our team. Stacey’s significant experience strengthens our investor relations capabilities and adds a new set of relationships with investors and analysts,” said Peter Seibold, Chief Financial Officer. “We’re excited for Stacey to join us as VP, Investor Relations and build a world-class investor relations program.”

Before joining Lambda, Finerman served as Vice President of Investor Relations at Marqeta, Inc. (Nasdaq: MQ), where she built the company’s first investor relations function. She previously held senior investor relations roles at PagerDuty, Inc. (NYSE: PD), Eventbrite, Inc. (NYSE: EB), Block, Inc. (NYSE: XYZ; formerly Square) and Zayo Group, and began her career at Citigroup Inc (NYSE: C) before expanding her corporate credit expertise with Goldman Sachs Group, Inc (NYSE: GS), NatWest Group plc (Nasdaq: NWG; formerly the Royal Bank of Scotland), and ING Investment Management. Finerman's expertise spans investor relations, financial strategy, and corporate development across both growth-stage and public companies. Beyond her corporate roles, Finerman serves on the Advisory Board of Next Ventures, a venture capital firm focused on sports, fitness, and wellness. 

“Growing the infrastructure necessary to unlock AI’s potential is a significant opportunity, and the investor community is eager to understand and play a role in it," said Finerman. "I’m thrilled to join a team with the caliber of AI talent and depth at Lambda as we take on one of the defining challenges of our time."

Lambda continues to expand its leadership team as demand for AI infrastructure accelerates. As VP, Investor Relations, Finerman will lead Lambda’s engagement with the investment community, communicate the company’s financial and strategic vision, and strengthen transparency around its growth plans and business fundamentals as Lambda scales. Her appointment reflects Lambda’s focus on building the financial and operational foundation to support sustained growth for the supercomputers that power superintelligence.
