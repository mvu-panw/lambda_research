# Security Transparency Is One of Our Superpowers: Introducing Lambda's Customer Trust Portal

 May 2, 2025

 • 1 min read

We recently dropped a new Security page, consolidating our existing information in one spot; but we didn't stop there: we partnered with Safebase by DRATA to launch a fully-fledged Customer Trust Portal (trust.lambda.ai) in record time.

This portal is our transparency power move. It's a one-stop shop for customers and prospects to access our security docs, certifications, and related materials. Think security audit reports, compliance frameworks, and policy documents—all in one place. You can request access to:

- Lambda's SOC 2 Type II report

- The most recent pentest reports (we like to stress-test our systems)

- Summaries of our policy documents covering Data Management, Encryption Standards, Incident Response, and more (our security playbook)

We've organized the info by themes like Data Security, Data Privacy, and Access Control: click on a theme, and you'll get an overview of our posture, plus related docs and links.

Our Customer Trust Portal is a significant step forward in our commitment to transparency and trust. We're not just talking the talk; we're walking the walk. 

Take a look around trust.lambda.ai and see how we're building trust through transparency. Your security is our top priority—no ifs, ands, or buts.
