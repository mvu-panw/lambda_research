# Will YOU win Lambda’s Golden Ticket in October?!

 October 3, 2024

 • 1 min read

## A Golden Ticket to an extraordinary prize

We’re excited to introduce Lambda’s Golden Ticket prize draw, offering you and your team the chance to win full-time access, for six months, to a 96GB VRAM NVIDIA GPU instance – absolutely free!

## How to participate

- Eligibility: You must be a direct customer of Lambda, signed-up to our Cloud with a business email domain, and located in the US. 

- Earn tickets:

- Spend $100 or more in our Public Cloud on any NVIDIA H100 Tensor Core instance in October and earn 1 ticket.

- Spend $1,000 or more in our Public Cloud on any NVIDIA H100 Tensor Core instance in October and earn 5 tickets.

- The Draw: We’ll pick 1 winning ticket from all eligible tickets at the end of October.

- Fraud exclusion: Only accounts successfully invoiced for their October usage are eligible.

- Bring your teammates to boost your chances: The draw is measured at the account level, so bring your peers in your account, on the same business email domain, and accelerate your path to earning five tickets instead of one!

The winner will have a 96GB VRAM NVIDIA GPU instance assigned to their account full-time, for six months, free of charge - a value of $18,500. 

Terms and Conditions apply.
