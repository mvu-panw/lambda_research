# Lambda Builds AI Factories with Supermicro NVIDIA HGX B200 Server Clusters to Deliver Production-ready Next-Gen AI Infrastructure at Scale

 August 25, 2025

 (Updated on April 14, 2026)

 • 4 min read

- Expanded AI infrastructure with faster results with Supermicro's GPU-optimized servers

- Large-scale AI Factory for training and inference deployed in record time

- Supermicro’s advanced liquid-cooling helped reduce power and cooling costs, enabling energy efficiency and sustainability

San Jose, CA., August 25, 2025 – Super Micro Computer, Inc. (SMCI), a Total IT Solution Provider for AI/ML, HPC, Cloud, Storage, and 5G/Edge, announced today that Lambda, the Superintelligence Cloud, has deployed a broad portfolio of Supermicro GPU-optimized servers including NVIDIA Blackwell GPU-based systems to expand its AI infrastructure and deliver high-performance systems to its customers. This collaboration also includes Cologix's Columbus data center for deployment, offering Midwest regions access to enterprise-grade AI compute solutions.

"Supermicro is excited to collaborate with Lambda on powerful technology to push the boundaries of AI infrastructure," said Vik Malyala, SVP, Technology & AI at Supermicro. "Our broad range of GPU-optimized servers enables leaders like Lambda to deliver powerful, flexible, and energy-efficient solutions that can handle demanding AI workloads."

For more information about this collaboration, please visit Supermicro's Success Stories Website.

To meet growing customer demands for high-performing servers, Lambda selected a mix of Supermicro systems, including SYS-A21GE-NBRT with NVIDIA HGX B200, SYS-821GE with NVIDIA HGX H200, and SYS-221HE-TNR, all powered by Intel’s Xeon® Scalable processors. A key highlight includes the integration of Supermicro’s AI Supercluster with NVIDIA GB200/GB300 NVL72 racks that can handle a massive pool of memory.

“Lambda is on a mission to accelerate the path to Superintelligence, building gigawatt-scale AI factories for training and inference for the world’s top AI labs, enterprises and hyperscalers.” said Ken Patchett, VP Data Center Infrastructure, Lambda. “As we strive to create infinite-scale compute for our customers, the depth of Supermicro’s server portfolio is a valuable asset for meeting our present and future needs.”

With these advancements, Lambda was successful in bringing online a large AI factory, new energy-efficient servers with advanced cooling technology, and a unique architecture that helps Lambda deploy large quantities of next-generation AI accelerators in record time. 

"Columbus is a fast-growing hub for AI innovation, from manufacturing to healthcare. With Supermicro’s trusted systems and Lamba's customer base in the region, we aim to provide regional enterprises a powerful edge with low-latency access, dense interconnection, and the ability to move faster and scale smarter," said Chris Heinrich, Chief Revenue Officer of Cologix.

Together, Supermicro, Lambda, and Cologix are enabling rapid AI development across key industries such as healthcare, finance, manufacturing, retail, and logistics by offering the fastest path to production-ready AI and added flexibility to integrate with hyperscaler environments.
