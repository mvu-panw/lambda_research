# Lambda Named NVIDIA Partner Network Solution Integration Provider of the Year

 June 21, 2021

 (Updated on July 24, 2023)

 • 2 min read

Lambda today announced it has been selected as the 2020 NVIDIA Partner Network (NPN) Solution Integration Provider of the Year.

Lambda is a leading provider of AI-optimized hardware infrastructure to F500 enterprises, startups, government entities, and academic research labs. Lambda’s products cover a wide spectrum from the Echelon GPU cluster to the Vector deep learning workstation.

Joining the NPN program has given Lambda the opportunity to optimize our supply chain, reduce lead times, and better serve customers in the AI industry. Lambda utilizes the NPN benefits to accelerate the development of state-of-the-art AI infrastructure software and hardware.

The global NPN Program provides partners the expertise required to develop, deploy and maintain world-class accelerated computing solutions designed for today’s most demanding machine learning and AI workloads.

Lambda co-founder and CEO Stephen Balaban highlighted the importance of the relationship.

“Lambda is excited to continue collaborating with NVIDIA to bring world class AI systems to market. We’ve been able to work together on phenomenal deals over the years, providing AI infrastructure to some of the world’s largest and most productive deep learning teams.”

"Lambda Labs is a recognized leader in the HPC and AI market with their growing list of customer wins across industries, as well as their diverse reach and technical expertise,” said Craig Weinstein, Vice President of the Americas Partner Organization at NVIDIA. “Lambda Labs accomplishments this past year place them clearly at the forefront for NVIDIA’s Solution Integration Provider of the Year award -  we look forward to their continued success.”

Lambda continues to be committed to providing valuable hardware and software tools for training neural networks. Our recent expansion into GPU cloud services has allowed customers to spin up Lambda’s 8-GPU servers without a dime of capital expenditure. Lambda is extremely grateful to be recognized as the SIP Partner of the Year and looks forward to continued collaboration in the years to come.

About Lambda

Lambda's mission is to accelerate human progress with computation. Our workstations, servers, laptops, and cloud services power engineers and researchers at the forefront of human knowledge.

Our customers include Apple, Intel, Microsoft, Amazon Research, Tencent, Kaiser Permanente, MIT, Stanford, Harvard, Caltech, and the Department of Defense.
