# Lambda has a new address: lambda.com

 November 5, 2026

 • 1 min read

Your path to superintelligence stays the same.

Starting today, Lambda lives at lambda.com.

Lambda has been the Superintelligence Cloud since July 2025, and the rest of the world is catching on. lambda.com is the address we'll carry forward: a cloud for the teams training and serving the next breakthrough models, with compute that scales from a single GPU to AI campuses with tens of thousands of GPUs.

### What changes for you

If you're a Lambda customer, nothing you need to act on.

- Your services, account, contracts, and support stay the same.

- Existing Lambda links redirect to lambda.com, and existing email addresses keep working during the transition.

- You'll start to see lambda.com on our website, blog, and emails.

If, despite our best efforts, you notice something broken, report it at lambda.com/support.

### How to tell it's really us

Lambda was founded in 2012 by machine learning engineers, and it has used several domains as it grew. Here’s the full list of domains Lambda sends email from. Ultimately, all our communications will come from lambda.com.

### What's next

Two letters no longer describe what we're building: lambda.ai is becoming lambda.com. The address is changing. Your path, from your first prototype to superintelligence, stays the same.

Talk to our team: lambda.com/talk-to-our-team
