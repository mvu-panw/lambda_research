# Lambda Honored to Accelerate AI Innovation in Healthcare with NVIDIA

 March 19, 2025

 • 2 min read

Lambda is honored to be selected as an NVIDIA Partner Network (NPN) 2025 Americas partner of the year award winner in the category of Healthcare. This recognition is a testament to the incredible work done by our customers and partners at the forefront of AI-driven innovation in healthcare and biotechnology. 

We believe AI has the power to advance healthcare in ways we are only beginning to understand, and NVIDIA’s global NPN Program provides partners with the expertise required to develop, deploy and maintain world-class accelerated computing solutions designed for today’s most demanding machine learning and AI workloads.

From drug discovery to personalized medicine, AI is enabling researchers to analyze vast amounts of data, identify patterns, and uncover insights that would be impossible for humans to achieve alone. By accelerating the pace of research, AI is helping us get closer to new treatments and cures that will improve and save lives.

“This field is so complex that AI might understand it better than humans. Our challenge is to build models that can take us beyond our current limits—Lambda is helping us get there.” — Wojtek Swiderski, Principal Software Engineer, Genesis Therapeutics

At Lambda, our role is to empower these innovators. We believe that the true magic happens when access to AI resources is democratized—when researchers and organizations of all sizes have access to the tools and infrastructure they need to bring their ideas to life. That’s why we’ve worked tirelessly to provide solutions that scale, from individual workstations to GPU cloud instances and clusters, ensuring that our customers can seamlessly integrate NVIDIA accelerated computing and software into their workflows.

We are particularly proud of the work we’ve done to enable enterprise-grade AI platforms for clinical applications and to support breakthroughs in AI-discovered therapeutics, biomedicines, and drugs. We’re also proudly supporting the open-sourcing of key AI models, to ensure that the benefits of AI are shared widely and look forward to sharing more about this soon.

“The scale and stability of the Lambda cluster, accelerated by NVIDIA, helped enable us to train NeuralPlexer3. We would not have trained as large of a model if we had to use less stable, less scalable systems.” — Matt Welborn, PhD., Executive Director of Machine Learning, Iambic

“We thank NVIDIA for this recognition and for their partnership — in healthcare and beyond. With the general availability of NVIDIA HGX B200 on Lambda’s 1-Click Clusters, Healthcare and Biotech companies have even greater access to transformative computing to drive healthcare breakthroughs.” – Robert Brooks IV, VP of Revenue at Lambda.

“AI is advancing healthcare by speeding diagnostics, deepening patient communications and helping scientists discover new breakthroughs. Lambda’s NVIDIA-accelerated biotech solutions equip biotech researchers, labs and companies to accelerate their life’s work.” — Mat Torgow, Sr. Director - Healthcare Partnerships & Sales, Americas at NVIDIA.

This award is a reminder of the importance of collaboration in driving innovation. We are grateful to NVIDIA for their leadership in the field of AI and for creating a partner ecosystem that fosters creativity and progress. We are also deeply thankful to our customers, who have entrusted us with their AI compute needs: we are honored to be your partner in accelerating the future of healthcare.
