# Lambda selected as 2024 NVIDIA Partner Network AI Excellence Partner of the Year

 March 19, 2024

 • 2 min read

For the fourth consecutive year, Lambda has been selected as an NVIDIA Partner Network (NPN) Partner of the Year. In 2024, with our dedication to providing end-to-end NVIDIA accelerated computing solutions in Lambda Cloud and on premises, we were awarded the AI Excellence Partner of the Year.

Lambda, the GPU cloud company founded by AI engineers, is on a mission to build the #1 AI compute platform in the world, powered by NVIDIA GPUs. Customers choose Lambda for access to best-in-class NVIDIA infrastructure and machine learning engineering expertise for training, fine-tuning and inferencing of generative AI, large language models and foundation models.

Lambda’s hardware and private cloud business serves over 5,000 customers across manufacturing, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, financial services, and the U.S. government. Lambda’s AI Cloud has been adopted by the world’s leading companies and research institutions including Anyscale, Rakuten, The AI Institute, and multiple enterprises with over a trillion dollars of market capitalization. 

The global NPN program equips partners with the resources and expertise required to develop, deploy, and maintain world-class accelerated computing solutions designed for today’s most demanding machine learning and AI workloads. Our participation in NPN has contributed to many successes for Lambda, including becoming one of the first to market with NVIDIA H100 Tensor Core GPUs and NVIDIA GH200 Grace Hopper Superchip-powered systems last year.

“We’re honored to be recognized as the NVIDIA Partner Network’s AI Excellence Partner of the Year,” said Lambda’s VP of NVIDIA Solutions, David Hall. “The AI market moves fast, and our tight relationship with NVIDIA enables us to provide our customers with the specialized AI compute infrastructure, expertise, and tooling they need to swiftly implement and scale workloads at an optimal price-per-performance ratio.”

“As generative AI reshapes industries, companies are racing to adopt, deploy, and scale technologies that provide a foundation for AI-enabled growth,” said Craig Weinstein, VP of the Americas Partner Organization at NVIDIA. “Lambda is recognized as an NPN award winner for the fourth consecutive year for its expertise in powering customers’ most demanding machine learning and AI workloads.”

Lambda was founded in 2012 by AI engineers with published research at the top machine learning conferences in the world. Our goal is to become the #1 AI compute platform serving developers across the entire AI development lifecycle. We enable AI engineers to easily, securely and affordably build, test and deploy AI products at scale. Our product portfolio spans from on-prem GPU hardware to hosted GPUs in the cloud. Lambda’s mission is to create a world where access to computation is as effortless and ubiquitous as electricity.
