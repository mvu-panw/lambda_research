## SkyPilot: Kubernetes for AI Workloads made simple

Kubernetes offers robust orchestration for deploying and managing containerized applications, making it a powerful tool for AI workloads that require scalability, high availability, and efficient resource management. However, setting up a Kubernetes cluster can be complex, especially for teams without specialized expertise.

This is where SkyPilot shines: an open-source framework designed to automate resource provisioning, cluster setup, and workload management. It streamlines Kubernetes deployment on Lambda and reduces the complexity of running AI applications on Kubernetes clusters.

With SkyPilot, teams can efficiently scale their AI workloads, optimize resource utilization, and maintain control over their deployment environments, whether they are on public or private clouds.
