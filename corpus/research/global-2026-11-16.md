## Quebec campus (press release, Nov 16, 2026)

https://lambda.com/blog/worlds-first-large-scale-deployment-of-nvidia-photonics-switches-with-cpo
- 10,000+ NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs on NVIDIA Photonics CPO switches (144x 800Gb/s ports, liquid cooled, 18 external light sources) "at its Quebec campus".
- "142 MW of secured, nearly 100% renewable energy capacity, enables free cooling up to 80% of the year, and is guaranteed to a PUE of 1.2".
- Operator not named. QScale Q01 (Lévis, QC) advertises 142 MW and 100% renewable power (https://www.qscale.com/q01-campus). The match is inferred, not confirmed.
