## On-premise to cloud consultation

Lambda has ended its on-premise hardware business (Vector, Vector One, and Vector Pro workstations and Scalar and Hyperplane servers) as of August 29, 2025.

Warranties will still be fully supported. See our hardware documentation or contact support if needed.

We recommend switching to our Cloud products, which solve the growing challenges of power, cooling, support, and scalability with new AI accelerators.

Our team can help you migrate your workloads to the cloud: contact us using the form on this page.

If you still need on-premise AI hardware, we can connect you with Lambda partners.
