# Accessibility at Lambda

At Lambda, we believe web experiences should be accessible, usable, and inclusive for a wide range of people.

Last updated: July 2026

## Scope

This statement covers Lambda's public-facing marketing web properties. It does not cover the Lambda Console or other authenticated product experiences, which are subject to separate ongoing accessibility work.

## Our approach

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review and improve our web experiences to reduce barriers and improve usability for a broader range of users and assistive technologies.

Our goal is to align public-facing Lambda web experiences with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA where practical and appropriate.

## Areas of focus

Our accessibility efforts include:

- Keyboard accessibility

- Semantic structure and heading hierarchy

- Color contrast and readability

- Reduced-motion support

- Form usability

- Alternative text for meaningful imagery

- Responsive layouts and zoom support

As our web properties evolve, we will continue to identify and address opportunities to improve accessibility over time. We welcome feedback from users as part of that process.

## Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility issue or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at:

accessibility@lambda.ai

When reaching out, please include:

- The page or URL where the issue occurred

- A description of the issue

- Your browser, device, or assistive technology

We review all accessibility feedback and use it to inform future improvements.
