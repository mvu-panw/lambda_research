Lambda AI

"The Superintelligence Cloud". Account research and PANW positioning for technical enablement.

The 30-second version

  • Who: an AI-only GPU cloud founded in 2012. It is an NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud partner and seven-time NVIDIA Partner of the Year, with SOC 2 Type II. It runs AI factories from 5–75MW sites up to 75MW+ campuses. investors
  • Incumbent: Fortinet FortiGate provides the Private Cloud perimeter and VPN, with customers on FortiClient SSL-VPN. Fortinet removed that mode in FortiOS 7.6.3. docs
  • Recommendation: PA-5580 HA per Private Cloud, PA-7500 on the shared cloud edge, PA-3440 for management, PA-415-5G for OOB, and Prisma AIRS on BlueField for inference and head nodes. Nothing goes in the GPU fabric. details →
  • Why now: the forced VPN migration; a new CEO with a telco and infrastructure background; Vera Rubin buildouts (BlueField-4 "zero-trust security isolation", H2 2026); and hyperscale and pre-IPO customers pushing security scrutiny upstream.

Offerings

  • On-Demand: 1–8 GPU instances (B200, H100, GH200, and more) behind an L4 cloud firewall.
  • 1-Click Clusters: 16–512 H100/B200 GPUs on 400G InfiniBand. Multi-tenant head nodes, shared DIA.
  • Private Cloud: 1,000+ HGX B200, single-tenant, 1–3 years. Dedicated firewalls.
  • Hyperscale: 2,000–64,000 GPUs (H100/H200/GB200), 3–10 years.
  • Superclusters: 4,000–165,000+ GPUs, co-engineered, on 3–5 year terms. product
  • Government: US Naval Research, Space Force, Army, Air Force, and CENTCOM. Includes air-gapped Supercluster deployments and CRADAs. No FedRAMP is claimed. gov
  • Also: Managed Kubernetes, Managed Slurm, an inference API, and on-prem DGX/workstations.
  • Trust: SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001/27017/27701/22301. trust
