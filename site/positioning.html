Firewall Positioning

Performance first: line-rate threat prevention on every north-south link, with nothing in the GPU fabric.

Incumbent: Fortinet FortiGate on Private Cloud (perimeter and VPN), per Lambda docs. Customers reach their clusters over FortiClient SSL-VPN with a username and password, and SSO is turned off. Fortinet removed SSL-VPN tunnel mode in FortiOS 7.6.3, so Lambda has to re-plumb customer VPN access either way. That is the opening.

Why not the alternatives

Hyperscale Security Fabric (HSF)

A software firewall cluster (VM-Series/AIRS with a built-in load balancer) that reaches roughly 100–200 Gbps on ESXi under Panorama (PAN-OS 12.1.2+). It suits elastic, software-defined edges, but for a performance-first buyer with 100G/400G DIA, hardware gives more Gbps per rack unit. Keep it as the answer if Lambda wants security-as-a-service per tenant.

Anything in the GPU fabric

Inline inspection of InfiniBand/RoCE RDMA would add latency to collective operations and break the "unrestricted" design Lambda documents. Say this first, unprompted. It shows we understand AI infrastructure and keeps the conversation on north-south, where we win.

Talk track

Performance-first narrative

  1. "We never touch your GPU fabric." Every enforcement point is north-south. Training throughput is unaffected by design.
  2. "Line rate with everything turned on." PA-5580 gives 300 Gbps of threat prevention against 2×100G DIA. The datasheet number is the security-on number, not a firewall-only number.
  3. "One OS, edge to OOB." The same PAN-OS and Strata Cloud Manager across the PA-7500 shared edge, PA-5500 per-customer perimeters, PA-3400 management, and PA-415-5G OOB. One policy model for a lean infrastructure team across 15 named regions.
  4. "Your VPN migration is happening anyway." FortiOS 7.6.3 removed SSL-VPN tunnel mode. Moving to GlobalProtect (IPsec, SSO/MFA, posture checks) is the same project effort with a better outcome than a password-only client.
  5. "Sell security, don't just buy it." Lambda's cloud firewall is L4 port/CIDR only. PANW on the shared edge, and AIRS on DPUs, could become a premium tenant security tier for enterprise AI buyers who need it for procurement.

Objection handling

  • "Firewalls slow down training." Agreed, which is why none go in the training path. IB/RoCE stays untouched, and we size only the DIA edge, at line rate with threat prevention on.
  • "FortiGate is cheaper per Gbps." Compare security-on throughput, not firewall-only. Count the cost of an SSL-VPN CVE on a GPU cloud: CVE-2024-21762 (CVSS 9.6) was listed in CISA KEV in February 2024, and CVE-2022-42475 and CVE-2023-27997 were both exploited as zero-days.
  • "Our customers manage their own policy." Keep that model: each Private Cloud gets its own PA-5500 pair and the customer administers policy, matching the "customer-controlled policy" language in Lambda's posture doc. Lambda keeps platform guardrails through central management. (Confirm the multi-tenant admin design with a PANW specialist.)
  • "We're a lean team." One OS and one management plane across four tiers, with zero-touch provisioning for built-to-spec clusters.
  • "Government and air-gapped deals need something different." Lambda sells air-gapped Superclusters to DoD customers (Space Force, Army, Air Force, CENTCOM). PA-Series hardware runs fully on-prem, managed by Panorama with offline content updates. Bring in the PANW federal team on certifications.
