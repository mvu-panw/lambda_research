Sizing math

East-west: do not firewall

A 512-GPU 1CC is 64 nodes × 3.2 Tb/s, or about 205 Tb/s of RDMA bisection on InfiniBand. No firewall belongs in that path, and IB is not IP-routed through one anyway.

Lambda's own doc says compute nodes have "unrestricted access to each other on this fabric." Agree with that. It builds credibility with a performance-first buyer.

North-south: size here

Each node has 2×100G Ethernet, but internet traffic is capped by the DIA at the edge. The one published edge figure is 2×100G DIA (2024).

Inferred A Private Cloud HA pair needs at least 200 Gbps threat prevention with headroom to stay line-rate on both links. The shared 1CC/on-demand edge aggregates across tenants and sites, which is where the Tbps class fits.
